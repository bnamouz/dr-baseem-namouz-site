Dr. Baseem Namouz, pediatric specialist: ADHD assessment and support in Shefa-Amr

I have been a physician since 2011 and a pediatric specialist since 2015, and for more than five years I have been assessing and supporting children, teenagers and adults with ADHD. I also know ADHD from the inside: I was diagnosed myself only after I finished my pediatric residency.

I live with ADHD too

My ADHD was diagnosed only in adulthood, after I had completed my pediatric residency. I went through school, medical school and residency without a diagnosis.

So when a child, teenager or adult sits across from me and describes trouble focusing, getting organized or keeping at things, I listen both as a doctor and as someone who knows it from life itself. Personal experience does not replace a structured assessment, but it helps me ask the right questions and explain things in plain terms.

Areas of work

What I do

ADHD assessment for children and teenagers

A structured medical assessment from age 6: parent and teacher questionnaires, a one-hour assessment visit, and a MOXO test as part of the process.

ADHD assessment for adults

A separate track from age 18, with an online self-assessment questionnaire ahead of the assessment visit.

ADHD follow-up and continued treatment

ADHD follow-up visits and online requests to renew a prescription for continued treatment. Every request is reviewed by the doctor.

Care for children and teenagers

Pediatric care for children and teenagers at the pediatric clinic, 21 Tawfiq Ziad St., Shefa-Amr.

Background in pediatric cardiology

Knowledge and experience in pediatric cardiology.

Where we are

ADHD doctor in northern Israel: the institute in Shefa-Amr

ADHD assessments and MOXO tests take place at Magic Kids Institute, 9 Daher Omar Al-Zaydani St., Shefa-Amr. Dr. Baseem Namouz’s pediatric clinic is at 21 Tawfiq Ziad St., Shefa-Amr.

Families come to the institute from Haifa and the Krayot, from Tamra, Kabul, I'billin and Sakhnin, from Nazareth and the surrounding valleys, and from Akko, Karmiel and Nahariya. You open the file and fill in the questionnaires online from home, so you only come to the institute for the appointments themselves.

Frequently asked questions about the doctor

Does Dr. Baseem Namouz diagnose ADHD?

Yes. Dr. Baseem Namouz is a pediatric specialist who has been assessing and supporting children, teenagers and adults with ADHD for more than five years at Magic Kids Institute in Shefa-Amr.

What ages is the assessment for?

Children from age 6, teenagers and adults. Adults aged 18 and over have a separate track.

What languages is the visit held in?

Arabic and Hebrew.

Does Dr. Namouz have personal experience of ADHD?

Yes. Dr. Namouz was himself diagnosed with ADHD, only after completing his pediatric residency. Personal experience does not replace an assessment: the diagnosis rests on a structured process of questionnaires, a clinical visit and a MOXO test.

Can I come through my health fund?

Yes. The assessment process is the same for members of every health fund and for private patients; the fund only determines how you pay.

How do I book an appointment?

Open a file on the website and complete the questionnaires; once they are submitted, the institute team arranges a time. You can also contact the institute on WhatsApp: 054-402-0043.

Can we come for an assessment from outside Shefa-Amr?

Yes. Families come to the institute from Haifa and the Krayot, from towns across the Galilee, from Nazareth and the valleys, and from Akko, Karmiel and Nahariya, and you can come from anywhere in the country. Appointments take place only at the institute in Shefa-Amr; you open the file and fill in the questionnaires online from home before you come.
