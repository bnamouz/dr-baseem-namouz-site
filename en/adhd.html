Broad perspective

Understand the child, not just the label

ADHD can affect many areas of a child's life. Sometimes the difficulty appears in the educational setting, sometimes at home, and sometimes in social relationships, organization, persistence, concentration or sense of capability.

When questions or uncertainties arise, it is important to seek professional, structured and responsible clarification. The goal is not to rush to attach a label, but to understand the child deeply, examine the full picture, and build a suitable and correct path forward.

ADHD stands for attention deficit hyperactivity disorder; it is sometimes still referred to as ADD.

When to seek help

Signs to pay attention to

01

Persistent difficulty concentrating

Difficulty sitting for extended periods, following instructions or completing tasks.

02

High distractibility

Rapid switching between stimuli, difficulty maintaining sequence and a general sense of dispersion.

03

Restlessness or impulsivity

Increased movement, quick reactions, difficulty waiting or stopping.

04

Organizational difficulties

Forgetfulness, losing items, difficulty managing time or daily routine.

05

Gap between ability and performance

The child is smart and capable but struggles to realize their potential in practice.

06

Feedback from educational settings

Comments from school or kindergarten about attention difficulties, organization or behavior.

What the process looks like

Clarification, assessment and guidance forward

My approach in this field is based on a broad and structured evaluation, including getting to know the child and family, listening to difficulties, questions and goals, understanding the child's functioning at home and in educational settings, responsible professional assessment, and guidance forward according to need.

01

Professional in-depth diagnosis

Structured examination of the full picture before making decisions.

02

Family accompaniment

Providing explanation, structure and guidance to parents too, not just the child.

03

Personalized planning

Adapting the best path for the child according to their needs, environment and functioning.

Step by step

How an ADHD assessment works at the institute

The process is the same for every family and starts online, before you come to the institute.

01

Open a file

Complete the online registration form with the child’s and parents’ details.

02

Parent and teacher questionnaires

Parents complete the parent questionnaire and the teacher completes the teacher questionnaire. Both are required before an appointment can be set.

03

Scheduling the assessment

Once both questionnaires are submitted, the institute team arranges a time. Assessments take place on Wednesdays at 16:00, 17:00, 18:00 and 19:00, and each lasts one hour.

04

The assessment visit and next steps

At the visit, pediatric specialist Dr. Baseem Namouz reviews the full picture with the parents and the child and advises on next steps as needed.

Opening a file or submitting a questionnaire does not confirm an appointment. A time is set only after coordination with the institute team.

Computerized test

The MOXO test as part of the assessment

MOXO is a computerized attention assessment with visual and auditory distractions. It supports professional assessment as part of the diagnostic process and does not replace clinical evaluation.

MOXO appointments are on Tuesdays and Thursdays, 09:00–13:00, starting at 09:00, 10:00, 11:00 or 12:00. Each appointment lasts one hour. Submit a request; booking requires institute approval.

You can also request a MOXO test from home, coordinated with the institute team and taken on a computer.

Health funds

Health funds and payment

The assessment process is the same for every fund. The fund only determines how you pay.

  • Maccabi: no advance payment. Payment is made by swiping the child’s Maccabi card at the visit.
  • Clalit, Meuhedet, Leumit and private patients: payment is made before the visit. Once it is verified, a personal WhatsApp link is sent for choosing a time.
  • MOXO through Maccabi: free only for Maccabi Sheli members, with a valid referral from the referring physician.
  • The amount due is shown in the booking form before you pay.

After the assessment

ADHD follow-up and prescription renewal

Families already in follow-up at the institute can book an ADHD follow-up visit without registering again and submit an online request to renew a prescription for continued treatment. Every request is reviewed by the doctor. Do not start, stop or change medication without personal instructions from the doctor.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions about ADHD assessment

Who carries out the assessment?

The assessment is carried out by Dr. Baseem Namouz, a pediatric specialist and director of Magic Kids Institute in Shefa-Amr.

What needs to be completed before booking?

You open a file, then the parents complete the parent questionnaire and the teacher completes the teacher questionnaire. An assessment appointment is set only after both questionnaires are submitted.

When do assessments take place and how long do they last?

Assessments take place on Wednesdays at 16:00, 17:00, 18:00 and 19:00. Each lasts one hour.

What is the MOXO test, and does it replace an assessment?

MOXO is a computerized attention test with visual and auditory distractions. It supports the professional’s assessment as part of the diagnostic process and does not replace clinical evaluation.

Is there an ADHD assessment for adults too?

Yes. Adults aged 18 and over have a separate track that includes opening a file and completing an online self-assessment questionnaire ahead of an assessment visit with the doctor.

Is the assessment available to members of every health fund?

Yes. The process is the same for every fund and for private patients. The fund only determines how you pay: Maccabi members pay by swiping their card at the visit, and members of other funds and private patients pay before the visit.

Where is the institute?

Magic Kids Institute is at 9 Daher Omar Al-Zaydani St., Shefa-Amr.
