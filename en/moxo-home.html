MOXO from home

What to prepare

Use a desktop or laptop, working keyboard, stable internet and speakers. Chrome is recommended; minimum screen resolution is 1024×768. Open the link on a computer, not a phone.

Official MOXO information↗

MOXO from home

A suitable testing environment

Choose a quiet, well-lit room with a comfortable desk and chair. Turn off TV and notifications and put phones away. Prevent interruptions; do not coach responses or help with key presses. Follow staff instructions for supervising children. Report unusual fatigue, illness, interruptions or technical problems; staff will assess whether results can be interpreted.

Preparation supports consistent conditions but does not guarantee reliability. MOXO supports, and does not replace, medical assessment. Do not change medication without personal instructions.

MOXO from home

Next steps

  1. Submit a request; staff reviews suitability for home testing and coordinates instructions.
  2. Estimated cost: ₪800 plus 18% VAT — approximately ₪944 total. Arrange payment with the institute; this page does not process payments.
  3. After payment verification, institute staff arranges a personal link to open on a computer. Submitting a request alone does not issue a link.
  4. Complete the assessment as instructed; results undergo professional interpretation.
