Magic Kids Institute · New service

Who is it for?

From birth to before the seventh birthday. The form pack is selected by birth date and educational setting.

Magic Kids Institute · New service

How does the process work?

  1. Register online and save your personal parent questionnaire link to return to your draft.
  2. After parent completion and confirmation, a separate educator link is provided. Select no educational setting during registration if applicable.
  3. The system compiles the responses and generates the completed questionnaires for physician review, including signatures, consent and referral.
  4. Reports are for Dr. Namouz only. After his approval they can be emailed to his mailbox; they are not sent to Maccabi.

Magic Kids Institute · New service

Privacy and security

Personal links expire after 30 days. Each respondent accesses only their own form; answers lock on confirmation. Authorized staff review the documents.

This process is not a medical diagnosis — it is a structured compilation of reports for referral to the Maccabi committee, where the professional evaluation takes place.
