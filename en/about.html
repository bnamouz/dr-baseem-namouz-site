Professional background

Medicine that starts with listening

I am Dr. Baseem Namouz, a pediatric specialist who believes that good medicine begins above all with listening, responsibility and personal connection with the child and family.

Over the years I have accompanied children and families across a wide range of medical situations, with one clear goal: to provide professional, precise and humane care — care that does not focus only on the symptom, but sees the whole child.

For me, every medical meeting is an opportunity to pause, understand, ask the right questions, and tailor the right response to the needs of the child and family. I believe in clear communication, eye-level explanations, and clarity that allows parents to feel safe, calm and fully involved in the process.

My approach

Professionalism, sensitivity and personal guidance
